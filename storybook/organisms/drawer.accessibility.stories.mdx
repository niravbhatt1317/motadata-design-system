# Drawer — Accessibility

## What works

- Ant `a-drawer` → `role="dialog"`, **focus is trapped** within the panel and restored on close.
- It has a **built-in close ×** — a real, discoverable dismiss (unlike `MModal`, which has none).
  **Escape** also closes.

## Gotchas

- **Backdrop-click doesn't close** (`maskClosable:false`) — fine for forms (avoids data loss),
  but make sure the × / a footer Cancel is obvious.
- ⚠️ **No visible focus ring** inside the panel
  ([SF-001](../../findings/SF-001-focus-visible.md)) — important in a focus-trapped surface.

## Content

- Give the drawer a clear **title** naming the record/task ("Incident #1234", "Edit monitor").
- Keep the **actions footer** consistent: Cancel (left), the primary action (right).
